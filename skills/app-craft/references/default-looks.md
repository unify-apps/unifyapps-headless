# The looks every AI makes

Read this before you write the App look line's `not` part. It lists the
choices that show up in AI-made screens whatever the subject is. Each one is
fine when the request asks for it, and the request's own words always win.
When nothing asks for it, it is a default, not a choice, and a person who
has seen a few AI-made apps spots it at once.

This is a list to read and think with. No code checks it. Find the one your
first idea is closest to, say so in `not`, and change what still reads like
it.

## Whole looks

- A purple or indigo gradient into blue or cyan. It started as one CSS
  framework's default button colour, and models learned it from there.
- A warm cream page, a large serif heading and a terracotta or clay accent.
- A near-black page with one bright acid-green, cyan or orange accent, and
  glows around it.
- A newspaper look: hairline rules, square corners, dense columns.
- Frosted glass panels and blur, used as decoration.
- A colour left as the theme gave it: the default brand purple, a stock grey
  and a stock blue nobody chose.

## Type

- Inter, Roboto, Arial or the system font as the only font, picked because
  it is safe. Step 6 of [app-look.md](app-look.md) names the fonts to skip.
- Many sizes a small step apart, so nothing reads as a level.
- One word in a heading picked out in another colour, italic or bold.
- An ALL-CAPS label with wide spacing above every heading.
- A monospace font for small labels and numbers that are not code.
- Meta lines joined with middle dots ("A · B · C"), labels shaped "WORD —
  a fragment", and "→" added to every button and link.

## Layout and grouping

- Everything in identical rounded cards, one radius and one padding on
  every one, whatever it holds.
- A row of 3 or 6 cards, each an icon in a rounded square, a heading and two
  lines of text.
- A big number with a small label, more numbers beside it and a gradient
  accent, used as the first thing on every page.
- Cards inside cards.
- Everything centred: a centred title over a centred line over centred
  buttons.
- Every panel shown at once, with nothing one click away.
- Numbers 01 / 02 / 03 on things that are not steps in order.

## Colour, depth and detail

- The same soft grey shadow under every card.
- Pure `#000000` text or untinted greys, or a near-black picked by habit.
- Grey text on a coloured fill.
- A coloured stripe down one side of every card or alert.
- Gradient washes and gradient text as decoration.
- Emoji or plain characters used as icons.
- Placeholder pictures repeated across a row.
- Sparklines or progress rings standing in for real content.

## Motion and states

- The same fade-and-slide-up on every section, and a hover effect on every
  card, clickable or not.
- Bounce easing.
- A popup as the first answer to "show more".
- Only the full state built: no empty, loading or error state.

## Words

- Copy that would fit any product: "Transform your workflow", "Seamless",
  "All in one place".
- Labels named after how the system is built ("Webhook config") instead of
  what the person does ("Notifications").

## Where this list comes from

Anthropic's frontend-design skill and its frontend aesthetics guide, the
impeccable design skill (its craft floor and its rules for app screens), and
several 2025-2026 lists of AI-made UI tells, merged on 2026-10-05. App
screens differ from landing pages: people come back to them every day, so the cure for a default
is clear levels, grouping and one chosen accent, not novelty for its own
sake.
