# The story sheet

`F/story.md`, written at the hand-over; its head, files, decisions and flags
go in chat. The person reviews the outputs against it, so it says everything they say, where each claim comes
from, and what the loop decided on its own.

```markdown

**Kind:** <use-case story | product demo | overview | teaser> · **For:** <audience>
**Made:** <the HTML presentation, N scenes | the PowerPoint, N slides | the video, m:ss>
**Purpose:** <what the audience should do or believe after it, in one sentence>
**Look:** <UnifyApps light | UnifyApps dark | Vox | Kinetic | the theme's colours and fonts, each with where it came from>; what in the request or the material chose it when it was not asked
```
